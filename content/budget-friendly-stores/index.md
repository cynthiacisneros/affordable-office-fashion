title: Budget-Friendly Stores
---

Affordable retailers and practical shopping strategies for finding quality office clothing without overspending.

## Stores I check first

- **TJ Maxx** — good for discounted blazers, sweaters, handbags, and name-brand basics.
- **Ross** — helpful for finding affordable shoes, accessories, and last-minute outfit pieces.
- **Old Navy** — an easy place to look for work pants, layering tops, and simple dresses.
- **H&M** — useful for trendy pieces and updated colors without spending too much.
- **Target** — convenient for polished basics, comfortable workwear, and affordable accessories.

I always check the fabric, stitching, and fit before buying, because a low price is only a good deal if I will actually wear the item. I also use my [[../styling-tips/index|styling tips]] to make a few affordable pieces work in several outfits.
