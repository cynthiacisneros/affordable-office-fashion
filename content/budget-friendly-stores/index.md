# Budget-Friendly Stores

Affordable retailers and practical shopping strategies for finding quality office clothing without overspending.

## Stores I check first

- [TJ Maxx](https://tjmaxx.tjx.com/store/) — good for discounted blazers, sweaters, handbags, and name-brand basics.
- [Ross](https://www.rossstores.com/) — helpful for finding affordable shoes, accessories, and last-minute outfit pieces.
- [Old Navy](https://oldnavy.gap.com/) — an easy place to look for work pants, layering tops, and simple dresses.
- [H&M](https://www2.hm.com/) — useful for trendy pieces and updated colors without spending too much.
- [Target](https://www.target.com/) — convenient for polished basics, comfortable workwear, and affordable accessories.

I always check the fabric, stitching, and fit before buying, because a low price is only a good deal if I will actually wear the item. I also use my [[../styling-tips/index|styling tips]] to make a few affordable pieces work in several outfits.

## Shop with a plan

Before I browse, I make a short list of the gap I am trying to fill and a few colors that will work with what I already own. My [[shopping-with-a-plan|Shopping With a Plan]] page walks through that process.

## Check the quality

A low price is not helpful if the piece looks worn after two washes or stays uncomfortable in my closet. I use the [[checking-quality-on-a-budget|Checking Quality on a Budget]] checklist before I buy.
