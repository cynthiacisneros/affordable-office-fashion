# Transitional-Weather Workwear

The hardest weather for me is the in-between weather: cold in the morning, warm at lunch, and unpredictable by the time I leave work. I do not want a separate wardrobe for every temperature, so I use light layers and pieces that can handle more than one season.

## Use a removable layer

I start with a base that looks finished without a jacket. A knit top, blouse, or clean tee can work under a cardigan, vest, or lightweight blazer. That way I can remove the top layer when the day warms up without feeling underdressed.

A simple formula is:

**straight-leg pants + lightweight top + cardigan or blazer + loafers**

If rain or wind is possible, I choose a shoe and bag that can handle the commute and keep the rest of the outfit simple.

## Let color change the feeling

The same neutral pants can feel like spring with pale blue or sage, like fall with burgundy or olive, or like winter with cream and navy. I use [[../styling-tips/color-pairing-for-the-office|Color Pairing for the Office]] to change the mood without buying a new seasonal wardrobe.

## Keep a desk backup

A neutral cardigan, scarf, or pair of flats at my desk can save an outfit when the temperature changes. I also keep an extra layer in my bag when I know I will be outside early or late.
