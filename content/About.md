# About Me

Hi, I’m Cynthia. I created this knowledge base to explore how women can build stylish, professional office wardrobes on a realistic budget. I enjoy finding practical pieces that feel polished, current, and easy to combine into confident work outfits.

My goal is to keep the advice useful for real workdays: commuting, sitting at a desk, moving between meetings, and getting dressed without spending more than necessary.
