# Choosing Work Pants

Work pants are one of the pieces I use the most, so I would rather take my time choosing them than buy several pairs that never feel right. The best pair for me is not necessarily the most expensive or the trendiest. It is the pair I can sit in, walk in, and style with several tops.

## Start with the job they need to do

Before I shop, I think about my normal workday. If I sit for long periods, I want a waistband that stays comfortable after lunch. If I walk between buildings or commute, I look for fabric that moves and does not wrinkle immediately. If my office is more formal, I might choose a sharper trouser. If it is relaxed, a clean pull-on or knit pant can still look polished with the right top and layer.

## Shapes I would try first

- **Straight-leg:** easy to balance with blouses, knits, loafers, or flats.
- **Wide-leg:** comfortable and current, especially with a tucked or fitted top.
- **Ankle-length:** useful with flats and lighter layers, as long as the hem works with the shoe.
- **Pull-on trousers:** practical for comfort, but I look for a smooth waistband and enough structure that they do not look like lounge pants.

I usually start with one dark neutral and one softer neutral. Black, navy, gray, brown, or subtle pinstripes give me more combinations than a very specific color.

## My fitting checklist

I sit down, walk, and reach forward before deciding. I check whether the pockets pull, whether the fabric becomes transparent, and whether the hem works with the shoes I already own. If the pants almost fit, I compare the alteration cost with how often I will wear them.

I also try to picture at least three outfits before buying.
